**Rule: every MinMaxHR deliverable — video, image, deck, artifact — must carry the logo.**
Use `minmaxhr-logo-dark.png` on dark grounds, `minmaxhr-logo.png` on white/light.

## Type
**Inter** — 700 for hero lines, 600 for support, 400 for body. Tracking -1.5 to -2.5px on large type.
Inter is bundled by HyperFrames as a data URI, so it renders offline and deterministically.

## Voice

- Slogan: **With Minimum effort, Maximize Your Hiring Quality**
- Narrative anchor: *"When the resumes keep coming, who deserves my attention first?"*
- Memory sentence: *CandidRanker ranks and explains. My people decide. MinMaxHR keeps the evidence.*
- **First-person framing** — "my attention", "my people", "my organization". Use it at a few
  deliberate moments, not every line.
- Free tier: 100 resumes + 10 JDs. Growth: ₹9,999/mo. CTA: **Start Free**.

## Do not
- Claim autonomous rejection or "AI decides" — humans decide, the product records evidence.
- Position as an ATS or an ATS replacement; it is a decision-support layer.
- Show the logo before the payoff in a hook-led video (brand enters late, after the problem lands).
