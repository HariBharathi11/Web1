# Outreach Kit — Scripts, cadence, and objection handling

Rules that apply to everything below:
- **Personalise the first line or don't send.** One specific fact about them
  (their open job posting, their broken website, their funding news). Generic
  blasts get you marked as spam and burn the minmaxhr.com domain reputation.
- Send from hari@minmaxhr.com, 15–25 emails/day max (new-ish domain — going
  above this risks landing in spam for everyone, forever).
- WhatsApp only after a form-fill, referral, or a call — cold WhatsApp to
  scraped numbers gets your number reported and blocked.
- Every message ends with ONE ask, and it's always small: "worth a 15-min look?"
  Never "buy my product".

## F. Objection handling (the ones you're actually hearing)

**"80K is too much."**
Don't defend the price — change the denominator:
> "₹80K one-time is ₹6,600/month over a year. What does one lost customer cost
> you — one flat unsold, one admission missed, one bad hire? If the answer is
> more than ₹6,600, the site pays for itself with the first save. And if upfront
> is the issue, our SPP/monthly structure gets you the same thing at ₹7,999/month."

**"Others will do a website for ₹15–20K."**
> "Correct — and that's what they deliver: a brochure nobody finds. Ours ships
> with SEO + AI-search optimisation (ask them what GEO is — most agencies here
> can't answer) and an enquiry system so leads convert instead of getting lost.
> You're not comparing two websites; you're comparing a poster with a salesman."

**"Can you do it free / for exposure, and we'll pay if it works?"**
> "We don't do free builds — every client gets a team assigned and we keep only
> a few active projects so delivery stays at 14 days. What I can do is a free
> teardown of your current presence so you can see the gap before spending anything."
(Then actually walk away. Free-seekers consume the delivery capacity your real clients paid for.)

**"We already have a website."**
> "Great — is it bringing enquiries? Free 20-minute teardown: I'll show you what
> Google and ChatGPT currently say about you vs your competitors. If it's fine,
> you've lost nothing."

**"AI will make mistakes with candidates."**
> "That's why every ranking comes with a written explanation you can check, and
> your recruiter always makes the final call. It removes the reading, not the judgment."

**"We'll think about it."**
> "Of course. So I can plan slots — is this a 'not now' or a 'not sure it works'?
> If it's the second, give me one JD and 20 resumes / 20 minutes for a teardown,
> and let the result decide."

## G. Tamil-market note

For TN Tier-3 owners (manufacturers, schools, showrooms), open calls in Tamil,
keep the tech words in English (website, Google, ChatGPT, enquiry). Trust builds
in the first 30 seconds of mother tongue; the pitch content stays identical.
In-person beats every script in this segment — cluster meetings by area
(one day in Gandhipuram = 4 walk-in meetings).
