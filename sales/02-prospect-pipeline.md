# Prospect Pipeline — Where the paying clients actually are

Goal: a living list of 100 qualified leads within 14 days, worked at
10 new contacts/day. Track everything in one sheet (template at bottom).

## Segment ranking (by willingness to pay ₹80K–1.5L/yr)

### Tier 1 — Staffing & recruitment agencies (Offer A: CandidRanker)
**Why first:** resume screening IS their cost centre. They don't need education
about the problem — they live it daily, and they bill clients per placement, so
a tool that speeds shortlists directly increases their revenue. They already pay
for job-board subscriptions (Naukri RMS costs them more than you do).

How to find them:
- Google Maps: "recruitment agency" / "staffing services" in Chennai (T. Nagar,
  Guindy, OMR), Coimbatore (Gandhipuram, RS Puram), Bengaluru, Hyderabad — take the
  ones with 10+ reviews (means they're active and have revenue)
- Curated lists exist — e.g. Hyring's [Best Recruitment Agencies in Chennai guide](https://hyring.com/blog/best-recruitment-agencies-chennai/) is literally a lead list
- LinkedIn: search "founder OR director" + "staffing OR recruitment" + city filter
- Justdial/Sulekha "placement services" listings with paid badges (paid badge = they spend money on marketing = they pay vendors)

Qualifying signal: 5+ employees on LinkedIn, active job postings on their page.

### Tier 2 — Funded startups < 3 years (SPP + Offer A)
**Why:** they have money by definition (someone just wired it to them), they hire
fast, and SPP's 14-day delivery matches startup impatience. Hyderabad startup
funding is up 45% YoY and Bengaluru had 165+ deals in H1 2026 — the pool refills weekly.

How to find them (all free/cheap):
- [Growth List — funded Bangalore startups](https://growthlist.co/bangalore-startups/) (500+ with funding data)
- [TopStartups.io India filter](https://topstartups.io/?hq_location=India) — newly funded AND hiring
- [StartupTalky weekly funding tracker](https://startuptalky.com/indian-startups-funding-investors-data-2026/) and [Entrackr funding reports](https://entrackr.com/report/indian-startups-raise-74-bn-in-h1-2026-as-cred-meta-deal-lifts-funding-12123065)
- Inc42 / YourStory weekly "funding roundup" posts — every Monday, add that week's seed rounds in your 3 cities to the sheet
- Your Apollo subscription (already budgeted in the ops sheet): filter founded-year ≥ 2024, employee count 5–50, city

Qualifying signal: raised in the last 6 months, 5–50 employees, careers page with open roles.

### Tier 3 — Non-tech SMEs with money (Offer B: ₹80K website + AI system)
**Why:** zero in-house tech, already spending on marketing, and your Tamil +
in-person advantage is strongest here. This is where your one existing client
case study does the selling.

Sub-segments in priority order:
1. **Manufacturers / exporters** — Coimbatore (pumps, motors, textiles — CODISSIA
   member directory), Ambattur & Sriperumbudur industrial estates (Chennai), Tirupur
   garment exporters. They sell B2B, need credibility sites + GEO ("when a German
   buyer asks ChatGPT for pump manufacturers in Coimbatore, you appear").
2. **Private schools, colleges, coaching institutes** — admissions season = urgency.
   NEET/JEE academies in Chennai & Hyderabad spend heavily on ads already.
3. **Hospitals, clinics, diagnostic labs** — doctors pay for patient-acquisition;
   "appear when someone asks AI 'best dental clinic near me'" lands instantly.
4. **Real-estate builders & promoters** — TN tier-2 builders (Coimbatore, Trichy,
   Madurai) have high ticket sizes; one extra flat sold pays for you 50x over.
5. **Jewellery, textiles, furniture showrooms** — family businesses upgrading to
   e-commerce-ish presence; strong in Chennai (T. Nagar) and Coimbatore.

How to find them: CODISSIA / CII TN / FICCI member directories, IndiaMART sellers
with paid "TrustSEAL" (they pay for visibility!), Justdial paid listings, local
business WhatsApp groups, and plain Google Maps ("manufacturer Coimbatore") —
open each website: if it's broken/ancient/missing, that's a lead.

### Tier 4 — Referral & community channels (compounding, start now, pays in month 2–3)
- **Your existing client:** ask this week for 2 introductions to businesses like
  theirs. A warm intro converts 10x better than any cold message. Offer them one
  free month of AMC per closed referral.
- **NHRD Chennai chapter & HR meetups** — CandidRanker demo audiences.
- **TiE Chennai / TiE Bangalore, Startup TN, T-Hub (Hyderabad), K-Tech/Elevate (Karnataka)** — SPP distribution: one accepted "perks partner" listing = recurring inbound.
- **Chartered accountants & company secretaries** — they onboard every new company
  in town. 10% referral fee for closed Offer B deals. 5 CA relationships ≈ a salesperson.

## Weekly sourcing rhythm

| Day | Action (60–90 min) |
|---|---|
| Mon | Add that week's funding roundup (Inc42/Entrackr/StartupTalky) → 10 startups |
| Tue | Google Maps + Justdial sweep: 10 staffing agencies |
| Wed | CODISSIA/IndiaMART sweep: 10 manufacturers/SMEs, note website quality |
| Thu | LinkedIn: connect + message 10 founders/HR heads from the sheet |
| Fri | Follow-ups (everyone at touch 2–4), update sheet statuses |

## Lead sheet columns (make in Google Sheets today)

`Company | City | Segment (T1/T2/T3) | Offer (A/B/SPP) | Contact name | Role |
Phone/WhatsApp | Email | LinkedIn | Source | Monthly spend on problem (₹) |
Status (New → Contacted → Replied → Demo → Proposal → Won/Lost) | Touch count |
Next action date | Notes`

Rule: **every lead has a "next action date". No blank dates.** A lead with no
next action is dead — archive it.
