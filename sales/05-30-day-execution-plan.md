# 30-Day Execution Plan — from today to the first new paying clients

Time budget assumed: ~3 hrs/day on sales (you), team keeps delivering.
Everything here uses assets you already have (live product, live Razorpay,
legal docs, one delivered client, GEO proof screenshots, stage demo workspace).

## Week 1 — Build the machine (no outreach yet except the referral ask)

**Day 1–2**
- [ ] Create the lead sheet (columns in `02-prospect-pipeline.md`)
- [ ] Send the referral ask to your existing client (script D in `03-outreach-kit.md`)
- [ ] Ask them for the case-study numbers + 2-line testimonial + logo permission

**Day 3–4**
- [ ] Build the 1-page case study (Canva) — before/after + one number
- [ ] Build the SPP one-pager — eligibility (<3 yrs), 14-day delivery, ₹7,999/mo, 5 slots
- [ ] Rehearse the CandidRanker demo from the stage workspace to under 7 minutes
      (upload JD → rank → explain ranking → recruiter report)

**Day 5–7**
- [ ] Source first 30 leads: 10 staffing agencies (Chennai), 10 funded startups
      (this month's Inc42/Entrackr roundups), 10 non-tech SMEs (Coimbatore/Chennai)
- [ ] For each: find the decision maker's name + one personalisation fact
- [ ] Verify minmaxhr.com email deliverability (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) before sending
      anything — one afternoon of setup protects every future email

## Week 2 — Open the pipeline

- [ ] Mon–Fri: 5 personalised emails + 5 LinkedIn touches per day (scripts A/B/C)
- [ ] Follow the cadence table religiously; log every touch in the sheet
- [ ] Book the free "teardown" calls (Offer B) and demo-on-their-JD calls (Offer A)
- [ ] Weekend: add 20 more leads (weekly sourcing rhythm)

**Target: 50 leads contacted, 5+ replies, 3 calls booked**

## Week 3 — Convert conversations

- [ ] Run every demo with THEIR data (their JD, their Google/ChatGPT results)
- [ ] Same-day proposal after every call — one page: problem, what you deliver,
      price (₹80K + AMC, or ₹9,999/mo), payment terms (40/40/20), 14-day timeline,
      the week-1 refund guarantee
- [ ] 48-hour proposal follow-up call, always
- [ ] Continue 10 touches/day on the pipeline behind the calls

**Target: 6+ calls done, 3 proposals out**

## Week 4 — Close and compound

- [ ] Push every open proposal to a decision using the "not now vs not sure"
      question (objection kit, section F)
- [ ] First close: announce it (LinkedIn post + ask the new client for referrals
      on day 1 — that's when excitement is highest)
- [ ] Apply to 2 startup communities as SPP perks partner (Startup TN, TiE
      Chennai / T-Hub) — the compounding channel
- [ ] Review the sheet: what segment replied most? Double down there in month 2

**Target month 1: 1 × ₹80K build signed (₹32K advance in bank) + 1 CandidRanker
subscription + existing client on paid AMC. That's ~₹48K collected and ~₹16K MRR —
burn is half-covered in month 1.**

## Metrics that matter (check every Friday, 10 minutes)

| Metric | Weekly target |
|---|---|
| New qualified leads added | 20 |
| First touches sent | 25 |
| Replies | 4+ |
| Calls/demos held | 2+ |
| Proposals out | 1–2 |
| ₹ collected + MRR added | track always, judge monthly |

If replies are under 4/week for two weeks: the list is wrong (re-qualify segment),
or the first line isn't personalised enough. Fix those two before touching price.

## Blunt truths (read when discouraged)

1. You don't have a product problem — the product is live, priced, and has legal
   docs most small Indian SaaS never bothers with. You have a **distribution
   problem**, and distribution is a numbers game you can win at 10 touches/day.
2. The people saying "80K is too much" are telling you they're not customers,
   not that the price is wrong. A staffing agency spending ₹50K/mo on recruiter
   salaries has never once said an ROI-positive tool is too expensive.
3. One good case study converts better than any discount. Invest in the client
   you already have before chasing strangers.
4. Recurring revenue (subs + AMC) is the only thing that ends the monthly stress.
   Every deal should have a recurring component — no exceptions.
