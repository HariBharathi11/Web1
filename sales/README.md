# MinMaxHR / HAB Business Solutions — India Sales System

This folder is the complete go-to-market and client-acquisition system for
HAB Business Solutions, covering:

- **MinMaxHR / CandidRanker** — AI hiring SaaS (₹9,999/mo · ₹95,990/yr, Razorpay live)
- **Website + AI system builds** — ₹80,000 one-time / ₹1.5L per year packages, with
  SEO + AEO + GEO integrated at first deployment and monthly optimisation on AMC
- **Marketing Agent** — inbuilt AI marketing agent, sold as add-on or standalone
- **SPP (Startup Priority Program)** — startups < 3 years old get priority delivery
  and premium marketing bundled into an affordable monthly package

## The one-line strategy

> Stop selling "a website" or "AI software" to people comparing you against free.
> Sell **outcomes with a monthly entry price** to businesses that already spend
> money on the problem: staffing agencies drowning in resumes, funded startups
> hiring fast, and non-tech SMEs (manufacturers, clinics, schools, builders)
> who already pay for marketing and salaries the tool replaces.

Start with `05-30-day-execution-plan.md` if you only read one file.
