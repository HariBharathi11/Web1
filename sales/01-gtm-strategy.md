# GTM Strategy — MinMaxHR + Website/AI Builds (Tamil Nadu · Bengaluru · Hyderabad)

## 1. The core problem you described (and the fix)

You said: *"Many are ready to get it free, not for money. They say 80K is too much."*

That happens because of **who** you're pitching, not the price. Anyone comparing
you to a free Wix site or free ChatGPT was never a customer. The fix is a
qualification filter, not a discount:

**Only pitch businesses that already spend ₹80K+/year on the problem you solve.**

- A staffing agency paying 2 recruiters ₹25K/mo each to screen resumes spends
  ₹6L/yr on the problem. ₹95,990/yr for CandidRanker is a 6x saving. Easy yes.
- A Coimbatore manufacturer paying an agency ₹15K/mo for a bad website + zero
  leads already spends ₹1.8L/yr. Your ₹80K build + ₹8K/mo AMC is cheaper AND better.
- A college that runs newspaper ads for admissions spends lakhs per season.
  A website with SEO/AEO/GEO that ranks for "B.Sc admission Coimbatore" is
  obviously worth ₹80K to them.
- A shop owner who wants a free website is not a lead. Politely exit in 2 minutes.

**Qualification question to ask in the first call:**
"What do you currently spend per month on hiring / marketing / your website?"
If the answer is under ₹5,000/mo total — disqualify, stay friendly, move on.

## 2. Three offers (keep them separate — never mix in one pitch)

### Offer A — CandidRanker / MinMaxHR SaaS (recurring revenue engine)
- **Price:** ₹9,999/mo or ₹95,990/yr (already live on Razorpay — use these, don't invent new numbers per deal)
- **Buyer:** staffing/recruitment agencies, HR heads at 50–500 employee companies, funded startups hiring fast
- **Pitch:** "Your recruiters spend 60% of their day reading resumes. CandidRanker
  reads 200 resumes and gives a ranked, explainable shortlist in minutes.
  One saved bad hire pays for 2 years of the tool."
- **Demo asset you already have:** your stage workspace has 16 jobs and 138 parsed
  candidates — build one polished demo flow from it (upload JD → rank → explain → report)
  and rehearse it to under 7 minutes.

### Offer B — Website + AI System Build (cash-flow engine)
- **Price:** ₹80,000 one-time (SEO+AEO+GEO included in first deployment)
  + ₹6,000–10,000/mo maintenance & monthly optimisation (AMC)
- **Buyer:** non-tech SMEs with money — manufacturers, hospitals/clinics, private
  schools & colleges, real-estate builders, jewellery/textile exporters, logistics firms
- **Pitch:** "Not a website — a system. Your customers find you on Google AND on
  ChatGPT/AI search (that's GEO — most agencies in India don't even offer it),
  enquiries go into a tracker, and an AI agent follows up. You already have one
  delivered client as proof (website + sales/employee tracking system)."
- **Payment structure:** 40% advance / 40% at preview / 20% at go-live.
  Never start without the 40%. The advance is the qualification.

### Offer C — Marketing Agent (add-on, high margin)
- **Price:** add-on ₹3,000–5,000/mo on top of AMC, or ₹25,000 setup + ₹5,000/mo standalone
- **Buyer:** any Offer B client after 60 days, or businesses with an existing decent website
- **Pitch:** "A marketing employee costs ₹18–25K/month. The agent drafts your
  posts, follow-ups and campaign copy for ₹5K/month and never takes leave."
- Never sell C before A or B is closed — it's the expansion revenue, not the door-opener.

## 3. SPP — Startup Priority Program (formalised)

Eligibility: incorporated **on or after 1 Jan 2024** (< 3 years as of 2026), verifiable
via MCA incorporation date or Startup India (DPIIT) certificate.

| SPP benefit | Detail |
|---|---|
| Priority delivery | Website + AI system in 14 days (vs 30 standard) — matches the "14 days" promise already on habsolutions.cloud |
| Premium marketing bundled | SEO + AEO + GEO + marketing agent included |
| Pricing | ₹7,999/mo × 12-month commitment (instead of ₹80K upfront) — first month + last month collected at signing |
| CandidRanker | 20% off first year if bundled |

Why 12-month lock-in matters: SPP converts your "80K is too much" objection into
"₹7,999/mo" — same annual money (₹95,988), zero discount actually given, and it
matches your existing annual plan price almost exactly. **SPP is a payment plan
disguised as a program, and the priority + marketing bundle is what makes it feel
like a privilege instead of an EMI.**

Cap SPP at **5 active startups at a time** — scarcity is what makes "priority" real,
and it protects your 2-person delivery capacity.

## 5. Proof & credibility assets (build these once, use forever)

1. **Case study of your one client** (1 page + 1 PDF): what they had before, what
   you delivered (website + sales system + employee assignment/tracking), one
   number ("enquiries handled per week", "hours saved"). Get a 2-line testimonial
   and their logo. This is your single most valuable sales asset — do it this week.
2. **7-minute CandidRanker demo** from the stage workspace (see Offer A).
3. **The GEO proof screenshots you already have** (ChatGPT/Google/Bing citing
   minmaxhr.com — they're in your Drive "Proofs" folder). Non-tech SME owners have
   never seen a vendor prove "ChatGPT recommends my business". Nobody else pitching
   them can show this. Lead with it in every Offer B pitch.
4. **One-page SPP flyer** (Canva) with the eligibility rule and the 5-slot cap.

## 6. What NOT to do

- No discounting below ₹9,999/mo or ₹80K — restructure payment terms instead (SPP, 40/40/20).
- No free pilots. Max concession: 14-day money-back window (your refund policy already supports ~90% refunds in week 1 — that IS the trial, framed as a guarantee).
- No custom features promised in the sales call. "That's on the roadmap" and move on.
- Don't chase leads that ghost twice. Follow-up cadence in `03-outreach-kit.md` — 5 touches, then archive.
