## 2. Break-even: three ways to get there

| Path | What it takes |
|---|---|
| SaaS only | **5 × CandidRanker subs** at ₹9,999/mo = ₹49,995/mo |
| Projects only | **2 × ₹80K website builds per quarter** ≈ ₹53K/mo average |
| Mixed (recommended) | 2 SaaS subs (₹20K) + 3 AMC clients (₹18K) + 1 build/quarter (₹27K avg) ≈ ₹65K/mo |

**The mixed path is the strategy.** Builds bring cash now; SaaS + AMC build the
floor so that by month 6 your fixed costs are covered by recurring revenue
*before* you sell anything that month. That's when this stops being stressful.

## 5. Pricing discipline rules

1. Never quote below ₹9,999/mo (SaaS) or ₹80K (build). Move payment terms, not price.
2. 40/40/20 on every build. The 40% advance IS the qualification test.
3. AMC is not optional in the proposal — bake it in ("all builds include monthly
   optimisation at ₹8K/mo, 3-month minimum"). Clients who refuse AMC become
   support burdens who call you free — price that in or let them go.
4. Referral fee (10% to CAs/partners) comes out of margin happily — a referred
   deal costs you nothing to acquire.
5. Investor's ₹11,500 and salaries come out of the *recurring* bucket first every
   month. Project cash is for tools, then buffer. Never spend the AMC money —
   that's the floor.

## 6. The "upgrade Claude plan" trigger you asked about

When MRR ≥ ₹55K for two consecutive months, upgrade — the plan cost is then <5%
of recurring revenue and directly increases delivery speed. Before that, the
current plan + disciplined use covers delivery. Don't upgrade from project cash;
upgrade from MRR.
